🥛 Dairy Products Sales Analysis Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to
analyze sales performance for a dairy products business. The dashboard
provides a consolidated view of revenue, orders, quantity, customers,
product performance, platform performance, and sales trends.

The goal of the dashboard is to help identify top-performing dairy
categories and products, understand platform-wise sales performance, and
analyze order and revenue trends over time.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI -- Dashboard development and data visualization

Power Query -- Data preparation and transformation

DAX -- Measures and analytical calculations

Microsoft Excel -- Data source / data preparation

📈 Dashboard Analysis

1. Revenue by Category

Analyzes revenue contribution across dairy product categories such as:

Milk

Ghee

Curd

Paneer

Beverages

Butter

Cereals

The analysis helps identify which categories contribute the most to
overall revenue.

2. Revenue by Platform

Compares revenue performance across different delivery and e-commerce
platforms, including:

Zepto

Swiggy

Blinkit

BigBasket

This helps evaluate platform-wise sales contribution.

3. Platform & Category Breakdown

Provides a combined view of platform and product category
performance, making it easier to understand which categories perform
strongly on each platform.

4. Monthly Revenue Trend

Tracks revenue across the months from January to September, helping
identify growth patterns and changes in monthly sales performance.

5. Top-Selling Products

Highlights products with the highest sales quantity, including products
such as:

A2 Buffalo Milk

Cow Milk

Curd

Skim Milk

Malai Paneer

Double Toned Milk

Buffalo Cup Curd

6. Order Volume by Day of Week

Analyzes order quantity by day of the week to identify variations in
customer ordering patterns.

7. Day-of-Week Filter

The dashboard also includes an interactive day-of-week slicer, allowing
users to filter the analysis by selected days.

🔍 Key Insights

Milk is the strongest revenue-generating category in the
dashboard.

A2 Buffalo Milk and Cow Milk are among the leading products
by quantity.

The dashboard shows differences in revenue contribution across
platforms such as Zepto, Swiggy, Blinkit, and BigBasket.

Monthly revenue shows an overall upward movement toward September.

Order volume varies across days of the week, providing an
opportunity to understand customer ordering behavior.

The platform and category breakdown helps identify where specific
dairy categories perform best.
